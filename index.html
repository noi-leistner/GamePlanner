Welcome to GamePlanner+

Stop losing track of what you’re playing. GamePlanner+ keeps your whole gaming life organized in one place.


Organize your collections

Sort your games into collections like Currently Playing, Pending or Completed, or make your own. Give each one a name, a category and a description, and edit them whenever your plans change.

Log every session

Record when you played, how many hours you put in, and notes on your progress. See exactly how much time you’ve spent on each game, and never forget where you left off.

Explore 500,000+ games

Search by name, genre or platform and browse cover art, ratings and details for every game. Find something you like and add it to a collection in one click.

Ask GameGuide what to play next

Not sure what to start? Tell our AI assistant which genres you enjoy or how much time you have, and it will suggest your next game.
