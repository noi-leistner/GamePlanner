Frequently asked questions

Is GamePlanner+ really free?

Yes. Every feature is free forever, with no subscriptions or hidden costs.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Just open the app and start building your collections right away.

Where is my data saved?

Your collections and sessions are saved in your browser, so they stay there after you close it. Clearing your browser data will delete them, and they won't appear on other devices.

Where does the game information come from?

All game details, covers and ratings come from the RAWG Video Games Database, with over 500,000 games.

What is GameGuide?

GameGuide is our AI assistant. Tell it which genres you like or how much time you have, and it will suggest what to play next.
